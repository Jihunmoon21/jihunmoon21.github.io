Jihun Moon

I am a master's student in Cognitive Science at Seoul National University, where I am advised by Prof. Youngjae Yu at the Physical Intelligence Lab. I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Seoul National University in August 2026.

My research interests lie in World Models, Long-Term Egocentric Experience, and Personalized AI. I study how intelligence builds its own world model from accumulated experience — and how that experience should be organized — drawing computational hypotheses from human cognition.

Research
The Limits of Your World Model Mean the Limits of Your Language Model: Embodied Reasoning through a Predictive Action Boundary
Jihun Moon*, Yumin Park*, Hogun Park*, Youngjae Yu (* equal contribution)
Under review, 2026
[Paper]

We introduce EGO, a framework in which a VLM selects a task-appropriate action within a predictive action boundary proposed by a V-JEPA2-based world model — aligning language reasoning with action possibilities grounded in visual experience, and improving both single-step embodied reasoning and multi-step procedure anticipation on egocentric video.

SPREAD: Scalable Pre-trained World Model for Adaptive Dynamics Model
Jihun Moon, Seong-Woo Kim
IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), 2026
[Paper] [Project Video]

We introduce SPREAD, the first framework that enables a pre-trained world model to learn environmental changes online, and continuously.
